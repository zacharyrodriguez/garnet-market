Community rules & safety

Short version: be honest, be safe, and only sell what's yours to sell.

Who can use Garnet Market

Anyone with an active @union.edu email: students, faculty and staff. Your name and Union email are visible to other signed-in members so buyers can reach you.

Not allowed

  • Alcohol, tobacco, vapes, cannabis, drugs or paraphernalia
  • Prescription or over-the-counter medications
  • Weapons of any kind (including replicas), fireworks or hazardous materials
  • Items not allowed in residence halls under Union housing policy (for example candles, halogen lamps or open-coil appliances)
  • College property, such as dorm furniture, mattresses or equipment that belongs to Union College
  • Academic work: papers, exams, answers or homework help for graded work
  • Stolen, counterfeit or recalled items, live animals, or anything illegal
  • Housing sublets, services, and anything that isn't a physical item or event ticket

Listing honestly

  • Use your own photos and describe the condition, including any wear or defects
  • Mark items sold once they're gone, and don't post duplicate listings
  • Tickets must be resold at or below face value

Staying safe

  • Meet in busy campus spots like Reamer Campus Center or Schaffer Library, during the day if you can
  • Look over the item before you pay. Cash or a payment app with someone you've met is safest
  • Be wary of anyone who wants to pay or be paid in advance, overpays, or asks to move off campus
  • Never share your sign-in link or code with anyone

How moderation works

Use Report this listing on any post that breaks these rules. Moderators review reports and can remove listings. Repeated or serious violations can lead to losing access.

The fine print

Garnet Market is a student-run project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Union College, and it isn't a party to any sale. Buyers and sellers are responsible for their own transactions and must follow the college's student handbook.
